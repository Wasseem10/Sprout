## 30–60 minutes: physical voice rehearsal

Use the actual laptop microphone, headphones/speakers and desktop buddy. Follow `COMPUTER-DEMO.md` from its focused bill recording section. Keep keys/settings panels out of the recording.

1. Read the two sample PDF bills, grant consent, and confirm the numbers.
2. Ask to analyze the bills with Databricks. Show the real verification result and explain the $45 usage effect.
3. Set a $60 monthly limit, choose duct repair, and prepare the fictional practice application with demo details.
4. While the buddy is speaking, say: “Wait, I can only afford thirty dollars a month.” Confirm speech stops, the limit changes and the old review is invalidated.
5. Choose weather sealing at $25/month. Show preserved answers and updated browser form, then ask for read-back.

If microphone/echo is unreliable, fix that before recording. A typed fallback is useful for venue failures but must not be represented as a successful voice demo.
