Audit started from UI branch `ui/presentation-polish`, commit `c80412c`; upstream `f1423fd` remained unchanged when fetched on 2026-10-10 local time. This audit and its navigation/visibility corrections are delivered together in the following commit. The [draft PR](https://github.com/Wasseem10/insightos/pull/1) remains open and unmerged.

## External acceptance and conditional work

- **Live provider and physical audio remain unverified.** The current local `/api/status` again reports ElevenLabs `configured: false` and `ttsConfigured: false`. Historical upstream provider/user rehearsal records predate the final avatar/audio bridge; they do not verify this build. Completing this check needs the existing integration configured locally plus a real microphone/speaker rehearsal. No credential values were read or recorded. The prior user question about configuring locally versus handing this check to the cousin remains unanswered.
- **Physical assistive-technology and other displays are not certified.** AX-tree, keyboard, forced-colors, 320 CSS-pixel reflow and native 200% zoom are useful direct evidence of those mechanisms. They do not establish a human screen-reader session or other physical monitor/DPI configurations. Current native reports name the tested work area.
- **Presented frames are a separate measurement.** The native panel trace records Chromium `AnimationFrame::Presentation` feedback, with complete start/end markers and a retained timestamp CSV. The trace omits physical scanout clock/completion flags and does not identify the first changed pixel frame for each input. Consequently, physical presentation and the strict event-to-two-presented-frame response gate remain unverified. rAF/render-call clocks, Chromium feedback and physical display evidence are kept distinct.
- **Deployment-specific binary checks follow deployment authorization.** Local compiled/public-build assets have matching binary headers and hashes. The existing public deployment predates Sprout. Production deployment and merging are separate user decisions under the master brief; no new deployment is assumed or initiated by this audit.
- **Conditional additions are not missing current-host requirements.** R3F/Drei/Next packaging is explicitly deferred by the master brief. Mixamo/VRM/three-vrm do not fit the chosen custom rig. Accessories/contact, paid/generated geometry, GLSL fluid effects, physics, visemes, music/system-audio capture, extra timeline/state libraries and compression are conditional on a demonstrated need. The current opaque glossy vertex-color GLB requires no texture transcode or decoder library.

The goal is not claimed complete while required evidence remains incomplete. This ledger preserves the full completion contract rather than redefining a local fixture pass as end-to-end success.
