Normalize pointer coordinates against the untransformed surface, not the moving plane. Sprout's current gaze basis uses the avatar's getBoundingClientRect; account explicitly for the parent perspective so panel tilt cannot feed back into avatar attention or introduce jitter. Use an untransformed layout-slot basis and preserve existing bounded 3D avatar movement.

Use a frame-coalesced, time-based easing update through refs/CSS properties; avoid per-move React rerenders. Return smoothly to neutral on pointer exit. Clear pressed/frozen state on pointer cancellation or lost capture, and reset on window blur. Keep new listeners/animation frames bounded and cancel them when settled, hidden, offscreen, minimized, unmounted or paused. Track only pointer events in the app's owned surface; no new global OS polling or gyroscope.

The response must not reveal a thick frame or light the entire perimeter. Prefer translating a prepainted decorative layer over animating blur or rebuilding large gradients. No pulsing brightness, rainbow scan, elastic page wobble or perpetual animation.

Keep interactions stable:

- Settle to neutral for text editing, keyboard navigation and open menus/dialogs. Freeze during native window dragging and settle afterward; reset on app blur.
- Preserve native drag regions, actual pointer hit testing, focus outlines, scroll position, textarea selection and drafts.
- Use hover-capable fine pointers only. Touch/coarse pointers, reduced motion and the existing motion-pause control receive a static panel. Dynamic preference changes take effect immediately.
- No native window resize/position IPC on pointer movement. Preserve work-area clamping and native topmost behavior.

The native shell has only 8px padding. Do not assume a new shadow fits because rotation is small: the existing shadow already exceeds that space. Bound additional movement/spread and inspect transformed corners, focus rings and controls at all work-area edges and 200% zoom. Do not widen the native window just to hide a clipping defect.
