# Sprout thin glass and pointer depth

Implementation study, 2026-10-11. Completed browser checks and remaining native gates are distinguished below. The historical `GLASS-PANEL-V005-STUDY.md` describes the previous thick-rim delivery, not the new eye asset version.

## Material and source treatment

The real panel is 392×656 CSS pixels inside the existing 408×672 native window, with eight pixels of layout/focus clearance. `buddy.css` replaces the broad seven-pixel painted band and concentric outlines with sparse one-pixel reflections. A translucent sheet admits background colour; local transcript plates and the composer preserve sharp readable HTML. The dense Tools well remains opaque to prevent transcript ghosting. Primary controls have deeper blue glass, fine cyan/lavender/coral highlights and an opposing inset shade; secondary controls are quieter. Stop keeps its red semantic cue. Mic/Pause remain 44×44 with zero padding and centred SVGs.

It uses no upstream displacement bitmap, shader, mouse deformation, React-state loop or live DOM copy. Its [required license notice](LIQUID-GLASS-REFERENCE-LICENSE.txt) is retained.

The user's [pill reference](references/user-glass-buttons-2026-10-11.jpg) guides selective bright edges and a concave reflection. Its checkerboard is a transparency preview, not product texture. The large panel stays quieter and almost borderless; controls keep their existing distinct shapes.

## Transform ownership and interaction

`DesktopBuddy` keeps `.buddy` untransformed. A relative `.buddy-visual-plane` wraps the header, decoration and conversation without remounting their refs. `useBuddyTilt` owns only that parent's rotation and prepainted reflection/contact-shadow offsets. `useBuddyPanel` retains its `panel.shell` transform/opacity, native acknowledgment, reversal timing, focus and reading-position memory. The native settings dialog remains a sibling outside the plane. Visible DesktopBuddy identity is Sprout; IPC/storage names are unchanged.

The plane uses perspective 1800px, yaw≤1.2°, pitch≤0.45°, scale 1 and no pointer translation. Pointer normalization reads only the fixed surface. The renderer owner supplies the avatar's neutral layout slot so projected DOM bounds cannot feed back into gaze. A held DOM press freezes the visible pose ahead of focus neutralization; release/cancellation settles appropriately. Editing, keyboard use, selections, scrolling, menus and dialogs neutralize tilt. Paused/reduced/coarse-pointer/forced-colour, native hidden/minimized and offscreen states reset immediately. All listeners, observers, timers and settling frames are disposed; no new provider, microphone, task owner or native pointer-position IPC is introduced.

Native dragging is a separate gate: [Electron draggable regions](https://www.electronjs.org/docs/latest/tutorial/custom-window-interactions#custom-draggable-regions) suppress pointer events, so DOM press coverage cannot establish title-region drag freezing. The native bridge now publishes read-only, versioned begin/end state from Windows [WM_ENTERSIZEMOVE](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/winmsg/wm-entersizemove) and [WM_EXITSIZEMOVE](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/winmsg/wm-exitsizemove), resetting on hide/minimize/blur. `desktopDrag` subscribes before fetching initial truth, rejects stale/disposed snapshots and disposes its shared listener. The tilt hook preserves a native hold through DOM release/cancel, stops its frame, and settles neutral on native exit. Hidden/reduced states still neutralize immediately. Other platforms return inactive drag truth; no unsupported native-drag claim is made. The actual registered Windows callbacks pass labelled fixture freeze/release and close/hide/show hook-retention checks. Physical title dragging remains **unverified**: the [bounded native diagnostic](../../../artifacts/glass-sprout-v005-native-drag-debug/DIAGNOSIS.md) did not establish a completed physical begin/end pair and does not distinguish input-injection failure from native hit testing.

Only transform properties of the plane and prepainted decorative layers move. Blur, filter strength and gradient construction do not change per frame. Scheduling stops once the target settles; this design is not a claim of measured performance. Normal and 200% native control/plane containment checks pass within the existing margin, with **zero resize events** from tilt.

The first active-tilt stills exposed a real compositing defect missed by the initial reduced-motion optical fixture: the coordinator retains its opening opacity animation with `fill:both`. That animation makes `.buddy-panel-glass` a backdrop root, so the old descendant `::after` filter could not sample the owned webpage. The correction moves the unchanged `blur(14px) saturate(112%)` onto the animated glass element itself; the pseudo-element retains its colour/reflection only. Animation/coordinator, sharp HTML, native bounds and renderer ownership are unchanged. Reduced-transparency/increased-contrast disables the filter on its new owner.

## Fallback and evidence limits

Reduced-transparency/increased-contrast preferences choose solid readable surfaces and controls. Forced colours remove author reflections and use system controls; reduced motion keeps a static plane. [Electron](https://www.electronjs.org/docs/latest/tutorial/custom-window-styles#limitations) limits CSS backdrop blur to webpage content: the material cannot frost other desktop apps. The earlier rectangular Acrylic study was rejected. No desktop capture is used at runtime. [MDN backdrop-root guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/backdrop-filter) also makes the transformed layered result a measurement requirement, not an assumption based on CSS declarations.

No physical assistive-technology or live microphone/provider session is implied by local fixtures, browser AX/keyboard, pixels or CPU tests. Controlled native screenshots require the existing fixture-pixel validation before publication.
