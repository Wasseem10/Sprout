Created **2026-10-10**. Status: **Prism selected by the user; Sprout subsequently selected as the character**. The three-way comparison is retained as provenance. Accessories and final layout remain open; no runtime or Blender changes are part of this study. See [PRODUCTION-WORKFLOW.md](PRODUCTION-WORKFLOW.md) for the current build order.

## Recommendation to compare

Our creative preference is **Prism**: keep the blue/aqua identity and introduce one broad, restrained violet-to-coral streak within the jelly. Keep the frontal face zone clear blue, the eyes navy and the cradle silver. The extra colors should feel like colored depth or soft light inside the body, not separate painted rainbow bands.

The current concept repeats blue across the body, glass rim, logo, composer and microphone, which reduces separation between character and interface. Adding a warm accent to the character while making the reading surface calmer can make the whole composition feel richer.

This is art direction, not a universal palette rule. Actual image comparisons and the user's response decide what stays.

## Layout proposal carried forward

- A recognizable floating pet that opens the compact conversation panel.
- A small identity/status header, one continuous readable conversation region, and a fixed composer with obvious voice/send controls.
- Requested details appear in a secondary view rather than a permanent dashboard.
- A luminous exterior glass frame with a stable nearly opaque reading surface.
- Most visual color lives in the selected character and frame highlights. Essential text stays navy or the corresponding high-contrast dark-theme text.
- Existing controls retain their real meanings. A colorful streak is decorative, not the sole signal for listening, success or error.

The reference concept's wide empty panel and extra slogans are not the final product layout. The portrait specification remains the starting proposal. Any final image/layout pass should show meaningful conversation content and a dark input label/placeholder.

The full implementation prompt will be consolidated after the final layout review, incorporating selected Sprout and Prism. The current MASTER-PROMPT.md is the earlier blue-led Ripple draft and is not the consolidated final prompt.

## Exact submitted concept-study prompt

### Output critique

The produced board gives Prism a coherent aqua/violet/coral sweep along the body sides and lower edge while retaining a mainly blue face. The neutral steel and soft colored reflections support the material separation. Lagoon reads fresher and more botanical; Ocean remains the simple baseline. These are candidate treatments for review, not selected runtime materials.

The generated interface samples still use a pale placeholder and decorative colored dots, so they are not final control designs. The Ocean sample's tiny buddy also picks up an unintended green/yellow accent. Judge each main character for the palette comparison, and verify consistent thumbnails and proper dark text in the later actual layout pass. The reference does not establish animation, glass contrast compliance or measured browser performance.

### Submitted prompt

Create a carefully art-directed side-by-side color exploration board using the attached InsightOS/Ripple image as the character geometry, face, polished steel cradle, product-render style and glass material reference. The reference is supporting design material, not a canvas whose layout must be preserved. Preserve Ripple's cute droplet shape, two dark navy oval eyes with white glints, tiny curved smile and substantial open stainless-steel ribbon cradle. Do not redesign the character, add limbs, change the camera between alternatives, or put metal across the face.

Landscape board with THREE equal vertical columns on the same bright nearly-white sky backdrop and identical broad softbox lighting. Each column has one large identical three-quarter hero character, one small compact glass assistant interface material sample below it, and one simple label at the top. Show true apples-to-apples palette changes with identical scale, pose, facial expression, reflection rig and composition. Minimal clean typography. Labels ONLY: A · Ocean, B · Prism, C · Lagoon. Interface labels ONLY: InsightOS, Ready, Ask me anything. No slogans, extra annotation, fake graphs or financial data. Render polished, beautiful and believable, original character.

A · Ocean: reproduce the vibrant electric-ocean-blue body and cyan translucent edges of the reference. Neutral polished steel; dark navy eyes. Cloud-white readable panel, cyan glass rim and blue microphone button. This is the unchanged blue baseline.

B · Prism: blue remains dominant, with a beautiful broad continuous asymmetrical COLOR STREAK embedded within the goo / jelly material, curling along its outer side and lower edge: aqua into soft saturated lilac/violet into a small luminous warm coral/peach end. Face stays mainly blue, so the navy eyes remain clear. Not separate colored ribbons, not stripes painted over the eyes, no full rainbow coverage, no rainbow metal. Rich harmonious colored depth, subtle mint/aqua highlight at one edge, elegant warm/cool contrast. Keep the steel neutral and let it pick up only faint reflected color. The glass assistant sample remains cloud-white with navy text, has a restrained aqua-to-lilac-to-coral gradient rim and a blue primary control. Only a small warm accent elsewhere. This should be the strongest thoughtfully colorful option, tactile and calm.

C · Lagoon: blue/aqua jelly with a graceful mint-to-lime inner side streak and a small soft golden/amber highlight near the lower edge. Keep face and eyes as in A, no yellow across the forehead or eyes. Silver steel neutral. Interface is cloud-white with navy text, aqua glass rim, mint/lime minor accents, blue primary control. Fresh nature/water-inspired optimistic Aero palette, with no oversaturated neon yellow.

High-end translucent 3D product renders with smooth stable geometry, rounded ribbon edges, rich jewel-like jelly, broad clean white reflections, gently grounded shadows. Saturated but controlled color. Each panel sample has a SINGLE glass frame, an opaque reading well, a visible dark placeholder and a small obvious blue mic control. Color is concentrated in the character and glass rim, not the reading text. No busy background, dark cyberpunk, excessive bloom, purple AI dashboard aesthetic, extra character variants, tiny illegible microcopy or decorative metrics. The board is a palette study, not a finished application screenshot.
